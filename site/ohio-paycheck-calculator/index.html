Ohio

Ohio paycheck calculator

In 2026 Ohio’s state income tax is 2.75% on nonbusiness income above $26,050. A personal exemption still comes off first, and it shrinks as income rises: $2,400 at lower incomes, $2,150, then $1,900, then nothing above $500,000. Joint filers are modeled with two exemptions. The state bill on a middle salary is modest. The city bill often is not.

Ohio cities tax qualifying wages where you work. Columbus and Cleveland are both 2.5% in this file, and a traditional 401(k) is removed from that municipal base, unlike Philadelphia. If you live in one city and work in another, a credit usually prevents full double taxation. This estimate applies only the city you select. It will overstate the total if you are quietly owed a credit.

Hundreds of other municipalities are not named. If yours is 2% or 1.5% or something else, put that figure in the custom local field. Do not leave Columbus selected just because it was the example. The state page starts with no city, on purpose.

Ohio income tax rules used for 2026

Ohio's state rate is 2.75% on nonbusiness income above $26,050, after a personal exemption that shrinks as income rises. City tax is separate. Columbus, Cleveland, Cincinnati, Toledo, Akron, and Dayton are named; other cities can be entered as a custom percent.

Rate: a flat 2.75% on taxable income above $26,050 for single filers. Income below that threshold is taxed at 0%.

  • Ohio does not subtract a standard deduction from income in this calculation.
  • Traditional 401(k) contributions reduce Ohio taxable wages in this model.

Details and simplifications: Flat 2.75% structure began January 1, 2026. Personal exemption is $2,400 at AGI of $40,000 or less, $2,150 up to $80,000, $1,900 up to $500,000, and $0 above $500,000, per person (taxpayer and spouse, plus dependents at the same tier). The joint-filing credit is not modeled. Municipal tax credit between two Ohio cities is not modeled; only the city you select is applied.

Confidence: published figures. Source: Tax Foundation, State Individual Income Tax Rates and Brackets, 2026. Last verified September 27, 2026.

Local tax

Ohio cities tax qualifying wages where you work. Columbus and Cleveland are named at 2.5%. Hundreds of other municipalities need a custom percent. Credits between a home city and a work city are not computed.

What is not included

Credits, itemized deductions, multi-state credits, property tax, sales tax, and unsourced local rates are not included. Employer payroll tables can differ from this annualized estimate. Ohio municipalities beyond Columbus and Cleveland are not auto-selected.

Questions

What is the Ohio state income tax rate in 2026?

2.75% above $26,050 of nonbusiness income, after the personal exemption. Income under the threshold is not taxed by the state brackets.

Does Columbus tax wages at 2.5%?

Yes, in this model, for people who select Columbus. It is not added to every Ohio paycheck.

Will two Ohio cities both be taxed?

Only the city you select is calculated. Real credit rules between a resident city and a work city are not modeled.
