Idaho

Idaho paycheck calculator

Idaho taxes wages at 5.3% after House Bill 40, starting from a standard deduction that matches the 2026 federal amounts ($16,100 single / $32,200 joint). The published table also shows a zero-rate threshold near $4,811 single / $9,622 joint; this estimate applies that threshold. Inflation-adjusted widths for 2026 were not fully available in the source table, so confidence is estimated.

Boise does not add a city wage tax here. Because the rate is flat above the threshold, reasoning about a raise is simpler than in a twelve-bracket state — and still not “5.3% of gross,” because the federal-sized deduction and the zero slice come off first. Federal brackets and FICA remain the larger lines for most salaries.

A traditional 401(k) reduces the Idaho base; it does not reduce Social Security or Medicare. If the zero-rate threshold is already baked into how your payroll starts the 5.3% calculation, this line can run slightly low — the methodology calls that out rather than hiding it.

Idaho income tax rules used for 2026

Idaho is modeled at 5.3% after the federal standard deduction, with the table's zero-rate threshold ($4,811 single / $9,622 joint) also applied. If that threshold is already inside the starting point, this line is slightly low.

Rate: a flat 5.3% on taxable income above $4,811 for single filers ($9,622 for joint filers). Income below that threshold is taxed at 0%.

  • Standard deduction: $16,100 single, $32,200 married filing jointly.
  • Traditional 401(k) contributions reduce Idaho taxable wages in this model.

Details and simplifications: Rate is 5.3% after House Bill 40. Tax Foundation footnote (j) says 2026 inflation-adjusted bracket widths were not available, so the threshold is the 2025 indexed figure. Standard deduction matches the 2026 federal amounts.

Confidence: an estimate (part of the state rule is simplified, as noted above). Source: Tax Foundation, State Individual Income Tax Rates and Brackets, 2026. Last verified September 27, 2026.

Local tax

Idaho does not have a local wage tax in this file. City costs show up in rent and sales tax, not as a second income-tax line on the stub.

What is not included

Credits, itemized deductions, multi-state credits, property tax, sales tax, and unsourced local rates are not included. Employer payroll tables can differ from this annualized estimate. This Idaho page does not invent taxes that are missing from the 2026 data files.

Questions

What is Idaho’s 2026 rate in this model?

A flat 5.3% after the federal-sized standard deduction and the table’s small zero-rate threshold.

Why is Idaho marked estimated?

2026 inflation-adjusted bracket widths were not fully published in the source table used here.

Does Boise tax wages on this page?

Not as a city income tax. The state line is the only Idaho income tax applied.
