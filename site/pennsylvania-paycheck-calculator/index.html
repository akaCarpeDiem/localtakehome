Pennsylvania

Pennsylvania paycheck calculator

Pennsylvania’s personal income tax on compensation is a flat 3.07% with no standard deduction. That is the state line, and it is rarely the whole local story. Thousands of municipalities and school districts levy an earned income tax. Philadelphia runs its own wage tax and is a separate page. Pittsburgh is in the city list as a modeled 3% resident rate and 1% nonresident rate. Everywhere else, type the percent from your Act 32 rate sheet rather than borrowing a neighbor’s.

A traditional 401(k) does not reduce Pennsylvania compensation the way it reduces federal wages. The deferral still cuts federal income tax and still does not cut FICA. If you max a 401(k) and expect the state line to fall, this stub will disappoint you on purpose. Section 125 health premiums are removed in this model, which is a simplification.

Select Philadelphia only if you live there or work there. A resident of the city pays 3.74% on wages generally. A nonresident pays 3.43% on wages earned in the city, on top of the 3.07% state tax. Leaving the city blank is the correct estimate for most of the commonwealth.

Pennsylvania income tax rules used for 2026

Pennsylvania taxes compensation at a flat 3.07% with no standard deduction. Traditional 401(k) deferrals stay in the Pennsylvania base. Local earned-income tax is separate — Philadelphia is not applied to the rest of the state.

Rate: a flat 3.07% on taxable income for every filing status.

  • Pennsylvania does not subtract a standard deduction from income in this calculation.
  • Traditional 401(k) contributions do not reduce Pennsylvania taxable wages in this model.

Details and simplifications: Employee retirement deferrals are taxable compensation for Pennsylvania personal income tax. Section 125 health premiums are still removed in this model, which is a simplification. Philadelphia wage tax and a modeled Pittsburgh earned-income tax live in the calculator’s local-tax list. Most of the other 2,000+ Pennsylvania localities are a custom percent.

Confidence: published figures. Source: Tax Foundation, State Individual Income Tax Rates and Brackets, 2026. Last verified September 27, 2026.

Local tax

Pennsylvania localities and school districts levy earned income taxes that vary by address. Philadelphia and Pittsburgh are named. Everywhere else, enter the percent from your Act 32 rate sheet rather than borrowing a neighbor’s rate.

What is not included

Credits, itemized deductions, multi-state credits, property tax, sales tax, and unsourced local rates are not included. Employer payroll tables can differ from this annualized estimate. Pennsylvania township and school-district rates outside the named cities are not guessed.

Questions

Does Pennsylvania tax 401(k) contributions?

In this model, yes. Employee deferrals stay in the Pennsylvania wage base. They still reduce federal income tax.

Is the Philadelphia wage tax included automatically?

No. Choose Philadelphia, or enter your own local percent. The state rate alone is 3.07%.

What if my township charges 1%?

Open Local details and enter 1 as a custom rate. It applies to gross wages and replaces any named city.
