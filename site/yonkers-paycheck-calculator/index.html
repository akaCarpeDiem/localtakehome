New York · True take-home pay for where you actually live

Yonkers paycheck calculator

Yonkers is not New York City. Residents pay a 16.75% surcharge on their New York State tax liability, which means the Yonkers line moves when the state line moves. It is not a flat percent of wages. Nonresidents who work in Yonkers are modeled at 0.5% of wages. The live-here switch picks between those two treatments.

You still owe New York State tax, federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, disability capped at $31.20 a year, and Paid Family Leave at 0.432% up to its wage base. Selecting Yonkers does not add New York City resident brackets. Someone who lives in the Bronx and works in Yonkers should use the city that matches residency rules, not both.

Because the resident surcharge is a percent of state tax, a traditional 401(k) that lowers New York wages also lowers the surcharge. A flat wage tax would not behave the same way. High earners should remember New York’s benefit recapture is not fully modeled past the 6.85% threshold, so the state piece—and therefore the surcharge—can differ from a full IT-201.

What this page includes

  • Federal income tax using the 2026 standard deduction and Rev. Proc. 2025-32 brackets
  • Social Security (6.2% up to $184,500) and Medicare (1.45%, plus Additional Medicare when wages clear the filing-status threshold)
  • New York: graduated state income tax (top 10.9%)
  • State payroll: New York disability (DBL), New York Paid Family Leave
  • Local: Yonkers (Yonkers resident surcharge / nonresident earnings tax)

Local tax

Yonkers is a surcharge on state tax for residents (not a flat wage percent), with a separate nonresident earnings tax when you uncheck live-here. Yonkers residents pay a surcharge of 16.75% of their net New York State tax. Nonresidents who work in Yonkers pay a 0.50% earnings tax on wages sourced to the city. The resident surcharge is not a flat wage percent, so it moves when state tax moves.

What is not included

Credits, itemized deductions, multi-state credits, property tax, sales tax, and unsourced local rates are not included. Employer payroll tables can differ from this annualized estimate. This Yonkers page does not invent taxes that are missing from the 2026 data files.

Questions

Is Yonkers tax the same as NYC tax?

No. Yonkers residents pay a 16.75% surcharge on state tax. NYC uses its own resident brackets.

What do nonresidents who work in Yonkers pay?

0.5% of wages in this model, when the live-here switch is off.

How does a 401(k) affect the Yonkers surcharge?

For residents, indirectly: lower New York State tax means a lower 16.75% surcharge.
