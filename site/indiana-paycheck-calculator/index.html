Indiana

Indiana paycheck calculator

Indiana’s state income tax is a flat 2.95% after a $1,000 personal exemption ($2,000 joint), down from 3% on January 1, 2026. A further cut to 2.9% scheduled for 2027 is not used early. The state piece is simple. The county piece is where Indiana paychecks diverge: every county levies a local income tax, and only Marion County (Indianapolis) is named in the calculator.

Open the Indianapolis page when Marion County is your locality. For Allen, Hamilton, or any other county, enter the certified percent from the Department of Revenue chart as a custom local rate. Leaving Marion selected for a suburban county will misstate the local line.

A traditional 401(k) reduces the Indiana state base in this model. Federal tax and FICA still dominate most salaries. Reciprocity notes can appear when you mark a work state; this page still taxes Indiana and does not prepare the other return.

Indiana income tax rules used for 2026

Indiana's state rate is a flat 2.95% after a $1,000 personal exemption. Counties add their own local income tax on top — Marion County and other large counties are in the local list.

Rate: a flat 2.95% on taxable income for every filing status.

  • Indiana does not subtract a standard deduction from income in this calculation.
  • Personal exemption: $1,000 single, $2,000 joint; $1,000 per dependent.
  • Traditional 401(k) contributions reduce Indiana taxable wages in this model.

Details and simplifications: Rate fell from 3% to 2.95% on January 1, 2026. A further cut to 2.9% is scheduled for 2027 and is not used here. The extra dependent exemption some families can claim is not modeled; the base $1,000 per dependent is.

Confidence: published figures. Source: Tax Foundation, State Individual Income Tax Rates and Brackets, 2026. Last verified September 27, 2026.

Local tax

Indiana’s local income tax is levied by county. Marion County (Indianapolis) is named at 2.02%. Other counties need the certified percent from the Department of Revenue chart.

What is not included

Credits, itemized deductions, multi-state credits, property tax, sales tax, and unsourced local rates are not included. Employer payroll tables can differ from this annualized estimate. This Indiana page does not invent taxes that are missing from the 2026 data files.

Questions

What is Indiana’s state rate for 2026?

A flat 2.95% after a $1,000 single / $2,000 joint personal exemption.

Is county tax included on the state page?

Only if you select Marion County or enter a custom percent. The state page starts with local off.

Will the rate fall again in 2027?

A cut to 2.9% is scheduled; this file keeps 2.95% for tax year 2026.
