Questions

Same engine as the homepage?

Yes. This page mounts the same 2026 calculator without the homepage explainer. Federal tax is an annualized bracket withholding estimate (Rev. Proc. 2025-32), not Pub 15-T tables.

Will local tax appear automatically?

Only when you select a city or county, or enter a custom percent. New York City is never applied to the rest of New York.

Does a traditional 401(k) cut FICA?

No. It reduces income-tax wages. HSA and Section 125 premiums reduce both income tax and FICA in this model.

Does this send my salary to a server?

No. The rate tables download once. The math runs in your browser. A shared link stores the inputs in the address bar, which you control.

Is this my exact paycheck?

No. It is an annualized educational estimate for 2026. Actual paychecks depend on your W-4, benefits, employer payroll system, and local rules.

Which tax year is this?

2026. Federal income tax uses an annualized bracket withholding estimate from Rev. Proc. 2025-32 — not Publication 15-T wage-bracket tables. The Social Security wage base is $184,500.

How to use the paycheck calculator

  1. Choose salary or hourly pay. For hourly pay, enter your rate, hours per week, and paid weeks. Weekly overtime hours use the multiplier you set, usually 1.5. For mixed rates, such as night differential or overtime on a differential, use “Add rate blocks.”
  2. Pick a pay frequency. Weekly gives 52 checks, biweekly 26, semimonthly 24, and monthly 12. The annual tax is the same either way. Only the size of each check changes.
  3. Set filing status and state. Filing status changes the standard deduction and bracket widths. The state controls the state income tax and any state payroll programs.
  4. Add a city only if it taxes wages. Choose a named city or county, or type your local percentage. Say whether you live there, because many local taxes charge residents and nonresidents different rates.
  5. Enter pre-tax deductions. These include 401(k), HSA, health premiums, and similar benefits. The stub shows which taxes each one reduces.
  6. Optional W-4 entries. These are the Step 2 multiple-jobs checkbox, the Step 3 credit amount, other income, other deductions, and extra withholding per check.

The result is a paycheck stub with every withholding named, a monthly and yearly figure, and a share link that saves your inputs in the address bar. Nothing is uploaded. The calculation runs on your device.

Why your real paycheck may differ

Employers use IRS Publication 15-T withholding tables, their own benefit setup, and the W-4 you filed. Some take deductions that this page does not know about, such as life insurance, wage garnishments, union dues, or a local tax that is not on the named list. If your stub and this estimate differ by more than a few dollars, compare them line by line. The difference is usually one of those items.
