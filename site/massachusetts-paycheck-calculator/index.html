Massachusetts

Massachusetts paycheck calculator

Massachusetts taxes wages at 5% after a personal exemption of $4,400 single or $8,800 joint. There is no large standard deduction sitting under that rate. The 4% surtax applies only to the slice of income above $1,083,150, and that threshold is not doubled for a joint return. Below it, the state math is close to a flat 5% minus a modest exemption. Paid family and medical leave is a real payroll withholding in Massachusetts and is not in this file yet, so the stub is light by that premium.

Boston does not add a city income tax on wages here. The missing PFML line matters more than a fictional city tax. Check a recent stub for the family-leave percentage and, if you want it in the total, enter it as a custom post-tax note in your own spreadsheet. This page will not invent the rate.

A traditional 401(k) reduces the income-tax base. FICA is unchanged. The raise calculator is a clean way to see the federal bracket and the flat 5% move together when an offer steps up.

Massachusetts income tax rules used for 2026

Massachusetts taxes wages at 5% after a personal exemption, plus the 4% surtax (9% on the slice) above $1,083,150. Paid family leave is not in this income-tax line.

Massachusetts brackets for single filers as used by this calculator, tax year 2026.
Taxable income (single)Rate
$0 to $1,083,1505%

  • Massachusetts does not subtract a standard deduction from income in this calculation.
  • Personal exemption: $4,400 single, $8,800 joint; $1,000 per dependent.
  • Traditional 401(k) contributions reduce Massachusetts taxable wages in this model.

Details and simplifications: The 9% bracket is the 5% rate plus the 4% millionaire surtax. The surtax threshold is not doubled for joint filers. Massachusetts PFML withholding is not in the calculator yet.

Confidence: published figures. Source: Tax Foundation, State Individual Income Tax Rates and Brackets, 2026. Last verified September 27, 2026.

Local tax

Massachusetts does not have a local wage tax in this file. City costs show up in rent and sales tax, not as a second income-tax line on the stub.

What is not included

Credits, itemized deductions, multi-state credits, property tax, sales tax, and unsourced local rates are not included. Employer payroll tables can differ from this annualized estimate. Massachusetts PFML withholding is not modeled yet, so the stub is light by that premium.

Questions

Is Massachusetts a flat 5% tax?

For wages under the surtax threshold, yes, after the personal exemption. The extra 4% applies only above $1,083,150.

Is paid family leave included?

Not yet. The income-tax line is modeled. The PFML withholding is called out as missing so it is not mistaken for zero.

Does Boston tax wages?

Not in this estimate. Use a custom percent only for a real local wage tax.
