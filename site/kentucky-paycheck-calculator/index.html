Kentucky

Kentucky paycheck calculator

Kentucky cut its flat rate to 3.5% on January 1, 2026, after a $3,360 standard deduction that is not doubled for a joint return (married filing separately is modeled with $6,720, matching the published note). The state piece got simpler. The local piece did not: many Kentucky cities and counties levy an occupational or income tax that is not in the named local list.

Louisville, Lexington, and smaller cities often show a local percent on the stub. Enter that figure as a custom local rate rather than hoping Louisville is assumed. Leaving local blank is correct only when you truly have no occupational tax.

A traditional 401(k) reduces the Kentucky state base here. Federal brackets and FICA still dwarf 3.5% for most salaries — until you stack a 1–2% local occupational tax on top and the combined bite looks less “low tax South” than the headline rate suggested.

Kentucky income tax rules used for 2026

Kentucky taxes wages at a flat 3.5% after a $3,360 standard deduction. The deduction is not doubled for a joint return.

Rate: a flat 3.5% on taxable income for every filing status.

  • Standard deduction: $3,360 single, $3,360 married filing jointly.
  • Traditional 401(k) contributions reduce Kentucky taxable wages in this model.

Details and simplifications: Rate decreased from 4% to 3.5% on January 1, 2026. Louisville / Jefferson and Lexington / Fayette occupational taxes are in the calculator’s local-tax list; other KY localities use the custom percent. Married filing separately is modeled with a $6,720 deduction, following the Tax Foundation note that the deduction doubles for separate filers rather than joint filers.

Confidence: published figures. Source: Tax Foundation, State Individual Income Tax Rates and Brackets, 2026. Last verified September 27, 2026.

Local tax

Kentucky can have local wage taxes in some places. This page leaves local tax off until you select a named jurisdiction or enter a custom percent.

What is not included

Credits, itemized deductions, multi-state credits, property tax, sales tax, and unsourced local rates are not included. Employer payroll tables can differ from this annualized estimate. This Kentucky page does not invent taxes that are missing from the 2026 data files.

Questions

What is Kentucky’s state rate in 2026?

A flat 3.5% after a $3,360 standard deduction (not doubled for joint filers in this table).

Why isn’t Louisville tax on the state page?

Occupational and city taxes vary and are not named in the launch local file. Enter your stub’s percent as a custom rate.

Did Kentucky just cut the rate?

Yes. It moved from 4% to 3.5% at the start of 2026. Older calculators may still show 4%.
