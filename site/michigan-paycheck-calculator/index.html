Michigan

Michigan paycheck calculator

Michigan taxes wages at 4.25% after a personal exemption of $5,900 single or $11,800 joint. There is no standard deduction beside it. The rate returned to 4.25% after a one-year dip and that is the figure used here. Most Michigan cities do not levy an income tax. A minority do, and Detroit is the one built into this file.

Detroit residents pay 2.4%. Nonresidents who work in the city pay half, 1.2%, which is the statutory pattern for Michigan city taxes. Select Detroit only when it is your city. A paycheck in Grand Rapids or Ann Arbor should not inherit a Detroit line. Other city rates can be typed as a custom percent if you know them; they are not guessed from a statewide average.

The exemption is per person in the published table, including dependents when you enter a count. A 401(k) reduces the state base. The Detroit model is grosser: deferrals stay in the city base, and the stub says so. FICA, as everywhere, ignores the 401(k).

Michigan income tax rules used for 2026

Michigan taxes wages at a flat 4.25% after a $5,900 personal exemption ($11,800 joint). Detroit, Grand Rapids, Lansing, Flint, Pontiac, Saginaw, and other major MI cities add a city income tax named in the calculator’s local-tax list.

Rate: a flat 4.25% on taxable income for every filing status.

  • Michigan does not subtract a standard deduction from income in this calculation.
  • Personal exemption: $5,900 single, $11,800 joint; $5,900 per dependent.
  • Traditional 401(k) contributions reduce Michigan taxable wages in this model.

Details and simplifications: No standard deduction. City income tax is separate. Detroit is 2.4%/1.2%; Grand Rapids and Saginaw are 1.5%/0.75%; most other named MI cities are 1%/0.5%.

Confidence: published figures. Source: Tax Foundation, State Individual Income Tax Rates and Brackets, 2026. Last verified September 27, 2026.

Local tax

Most Michigan cities do not levy an income tax. Detroit is named at 2.4% resident / 1.2% nonresident. Other city rates can be typed as a custom percent.

What is not included

Credits, itemized deductions, multi-state credits, property tax, sales tax, and unsourced local rates are not included. Employer payroll tables can differ from this annualized estimate. This Michigan page does not invent taxes that are missing from the 2026 data files.

Questions

What is the Michigan state rate?

4.25% after a $5,900 single or $11,800 joint personal exemption.

Is Detroit tax included for the whole state?

No. Choose Detroit on this page or use the Detroit page. Otherwise the city line is zero.

What do nonresidents working in Detroit pay?

Half the resident rate, 1.2%, on income earned in the city, in this model.
