Contact LocalTakehome

Questions, corrections, and privacy requests.

The best way to reach LocalTakehome is by email at support@localtakehome.com.

Report a rate or calculation error

If a result looks wrong, please include:

  • the page you used (for example, the Ohio calculator or the bonus calculator)
  • the inputs, or the share link from the address bar (you can remove the salary if you prefer)
  • what you expected, and a link to the official IRS, state, or city source if you have one

Confirmed corrections are added to the tax data, and the page’s “last verified” date is updated. The methodology page lists the sources and the known limits of the estimate.

Privacy requests

LocalTakehome has no accounts and does not store your paycheck inputs on a server. Saved scenarios stay in your own browser, and you can delete them by clearing this site’s data. For questions about cookies or advertising, see the privacy policy or email the address above.

Advertising and partnerships

For advertising or partnership questions, use the same email address. Ads and partners never change the calculator’s results.

Please note

LocalTakehome cannot give personal tax advice or review your pay stub, tax return, or IRS notices. For those, contact your employer’s payroll department, the IRS, your state tax agency, or a tax professional.
