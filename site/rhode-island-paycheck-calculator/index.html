Rhode Island

Rhode Island paycheck calculator

Rhode Island’s rates are 3.75%, 4.75%, and 5.99% after an $11,200 single standard deduction and a $5,250 personal exemption. Brackets are not doubled for joint filers. The deduction and exemptions phase out between roughly $261,000 and $290,800 of modified AGI — that phaseout is not computed, so high earners can see a line that is too low.

Rhode Island Temporary Disability Insurance is a real withholding for many employees and is not in the payroll file yet. Providence does not add a separate city income tax here. A traditional 401(k) reduces the income-tax base; FICA does not.

Cross-border work into Massachusetts or Connecticut is common. This page taxes Rhode Island when you open it; it does not allocate days or file the other state.

Rhode Island income tax rules used for 2026

Rhode Island rates are 3.75%, 4.75%, and 5.99% after a standard deduction and personal exemption. Brackets are not doubled for joint filers.

Rhode Island brackets for single filers as used by this calculator, tax year 2026.
Taxable income (single)Rate
$0 to $82,0503.75%
$82,050 to $186,4504.75%
Over $186,4505.99%
  • Standard deduction: $11,200 single, $22,400 married filing jointly.
  • Personal exemption: $5,250 single, $10,500 joint; $5,250 per dependent.
  • Traditional 401(k) contributions reduce Rhode Island taxable wages in this model.

Details and simplifications: The standard deduction and exemptions phase out between roughly $261,000 and $290,800 of modified AGI. That phaseout is not computed.

Confidence: published figures. Source: Tax Foundation, State Individual Income Tax Rates and Brackets, 2026. Last verified September 27, 2026.

Local tax

Rhode Island does not have a local wage tax in this file. City costs show up in rent and sales tax, not as a second income-tax line on the stub.

What is not included

Credits, itemized deductions, multi-state credits, property tax, sales tax, and unsourced local rates are not included. Employer payroll tables can differ from this annualized estimate. This Rhode Island page does not invent taxes that are missing from the 2026 data files.

Questions

What Rhode Island rates are modeled?

3.75%, 4.75%, and 5.99% after the standard deduction and personal exemption.

Is Rhode Island TDI included?

Not yet. Temporary Disability Insurance is listed as not modeled so it is not mistaken for zero.

Why might high earners owe more than this shows?

Standard-deduction and exemption phaseouts near the mid–$200,000s are not computed.
