Utah

Utah paycheck calculator

Utah taxes income at 4.5% after House Bill 106, but the standard deduction is not a subtraction — it becomes a credit equal to 6% of the federal standard deduction ($966 single / $1,932 joint at 2026 federal amounts). That credit phases out as AGI rises, using 2025 phaseout thresholds from the source table ($18,213 single / $36,426 joint). The effective bite is not “4.5% of gross.”

Because the credit fades, higher earners keep more of the flat rate and less of the credit. Confidence is estimated while those thresholds await a fuller 2026 update. Salt Lake City does not add a city income tax here.

A traditional 401(k) reduces Utah taxable income in this model; FICA does not. Compare Utah with Colorado (flat 4.4% plus FAMLI) at the same salary when a mountain-west offer lands — the shapes look similar until you stack Colorado’s leave premium against Utah’s credit phaseout.

Utah income tax rules used for 2026

Utah taxes income at 4.5%. The standard deduction becomes a credit — 6% of the federal standard deduction — that phases out as income rises. The phaseout thresholds used here are the 2025 figures published with the 2026 rate table.

Rate: a flat 4.5% on taxable income for every filing status.

  • Utah does not subtract a standard deduction from income in this calculation.
  • Traditional 401(k) contributions reduce Utah taxable wages in this model.

Details and simplifications: Rate is 4.5% after House Bill 106. Tax Foundation footnote (gg): the credit is 6% of the federal standard or itemized deduction (excluding state and local income tax) and phases out at 1.3 cents per dollar of AGI above $18,213 single / $36,426 joint, as of 2025. Those thresholds were not updated in the source table. Initial credit is $966 single and $1,932 joint, which is 6% of the 2026 federal standard deduction.

Confidence: an estimate (part of the state rule is simplified, as noted above). Source: Tax Foundation, State Individual Income Tax Rates and Brackets, 2026. Last verified September 27, 2026.

Local tax

Utah does not have a local wage tax in this file. City costs show up in rent and sales tax, not as a second income-tax line on the stub.

What is not included

Credits, itemized deductions, multi-state credits, property tax, sales tax, and unsourced local rates are not included. Employer payroll tables can differ from this annualized estimate. This Utah page does not invent taxes that are missing from the 2026 data files.

Questions

Is Utah a simple flat 4.5% of salary?

No. A credit equal to 6% of the federal standard deduction offsets part of the tax and phases out with AGI.

Why is Utah marked estimated?

Credit phaseout thresholds in the source table were still the 2025 figures published with the 2026 rate.

Does Salt Lake City tax wages here?

Not as a city income tax. The state flat rate and credit are the Utah income-tax model.
